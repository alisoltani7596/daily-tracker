Calorie optimisation

Diet
Protein first at every meal
Burns ~25% of its own calories in digestion. Lead with protein before carbs every meal.
Eat within 30–60 min post-workout
Muscles are primed post-training. Protein shake + banana accelerates recovery.
500 ml water before each meal
Reduces meal calorie intake by ~13%. Free reduction with zero effort.
No food after 8 pm
Late eating raises insulin, blunting overnight fat burning.
Cycle calories around training
+100–150 kcal carbs on Mon/Wed/Fri. Less on Thu/Sun rest days.
Workout
Never skip warm-up sets
Activates more muscle fibres — more calories burned and muscle built per session.
10-min cardio finisher on lift days
Jumping jacks after Mon/Wed/Fri adds ~80 kcal and extends afterburn.
Supersets on lift days
Push + pull back-to-back. Same volume, 30% less time, ~15% more calories.
Maximise NEAT daily
8k–10k Garmin steps = +300–400 kcal/day without any extra workout.
Train semi-fasted once a week
Tue HIIT on a light stomach burns fat as fuel faster.
